## Description (EN, draft)

Photo Cleanup finds duplicate and similar photos entirely on your
phone. Nothing is uploaded, there is no account, no ads and no
analytics — it even works in airplane mode.

- Exact duplicates and similar photos, grouped with a Best pick
- Screenshots, blurry photos and large videos, ranked by size
- Review every group before anything is deleted; at least one
  photo per group is always kept
- Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted for 30 days

Pro is a one-time purchase. No subscription, no trial, no auto-renew.
